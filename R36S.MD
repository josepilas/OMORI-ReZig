# Play OMORI ReZig on R36S

You need an R36S with PortMaster, a writable Ports partition, and your own supported PC game installation or Android APK. [Download `OMORI-ReZig-0.0.1-R36S.zip`](https://github.com/josepilas/OMORI-ReZig/releases/download/v0.0.1/OMORI-ReZig-0.0.1-R36S.zip)

## Install and prepare the game

1. Extract the **contents** of the ZIP into your card’s `ports/` folder. Check that `ports/OMORI.sh` and `ports/omori/` are next to each other.
2. Place one game edition in `ports/omori/original/` using one of the layouts below.
3. Put the card back in the handheld and open **OMORI** from the Ports menu. Give the first launch time and space to prepare the game data.

| Game data | What to copy |
| --- | --- |
| **PC** | Copy `www/` and its matching `Launch_OMORI.bat` together into `original/`. A complete game installation folder nested inside `original/` also works. A `www/`-only copy requires its matching data key in `omori-pc-key.txt`. |
| **Android** | Copy your own `.apk` into `original/`. Keep a separate copy of the APK, since the launcher removes the staged APK after successful extraction. |

The launcher prepares the game under `omori/original/extracted/assets/www/`. On a regular update you can keep the prepared files and launch again. If you change the game edition, keep a separate copy of the saves for each edition.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| D-pad or left stick | Walk or navigate menus |
| A | Interact, confirm, reveal text or advance a finished dialogue page |
| B or Start | Go back or open the field menu when the story allows it |
| X | Hold to dash |
| Hold Y and press left or right | Select the party leader when tagging is enabled. Release Y or press A to confirm. |
| L or R | Page up or down where supported |
| **Start + Select** | Exit |

The field menu unlocks after picking up the knife in White Space. Options is available from the title. Use the D-pad to adjust settings, then press B to save them.

## Update and keep your saves

Extract the new ZIP over the Ports folder. Keep `omori/original/`, `omori/saves/` and `omori/logs/`. Open **Continue** and choose your file to resume. If a save is recovered from its previous backup, the game shows a notice.

## If it fails to launch

- Confirm `OMORI.sh` and `omori/` sit together and that your chosen game data is in `omori/original/`.
- Read `omori/logs/short.txt` first. Use `launcher.log` and `extract.log` for preparation failures, or `runtime.log` for crashes and problems after the game opens.
- When reporting a bug, include the ReZig version, firmware, game edition and steps to reproduce it. Share a short excerpt of the relevant log. [Report an issue](https://github.com/josepilas/OMORI-ReZig/issues)
