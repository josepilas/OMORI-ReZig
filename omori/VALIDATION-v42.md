# OMORI ReZig 0.0.1 for R36S

R36S port by José Pilas. This release restores scripted camera pans and reaction balloons with their authored timing. It also improves the fallback countdown for MV maps with random encounter tables.

The build passed 235 native tests, PC and Android opening walkthroughs, and the ARM64 synthetic route under QEMU. The detailed results, comparison with the C++ reference, and porting guide are in `docs/` inside `OMORI-ReZig-0.0.1-source.zip`.

Keep `original/`, `saves/`, and `logs/` when updating. The previous v41 build was observed running on the R36S through Continue, the friends' room and North Coast. Physical testing of this build is pending.
